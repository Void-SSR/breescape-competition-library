The “Cozy in Hudson” shop in Hudson, N.Y., will feature the company’s proprietary Snuggle Sac , Cozy Bliss and Amelie Home collections. Although each has its own DTC online presence, they will be showing in a dedicated physical retail space for the first time. The holiday shop offers a comprehensive range of styles, innovative product designs, and advanced technologies, including Sunwin’s signature feathery yarn, faux fur, and a selection of heated items suitable for gifting. By engaging shoppers face-to-face, Sunwin aims to gather meaningful, firsthand feedback to inform future product innovation. The store will open Dec. 1 and run through Jan. 1, 2026. “Not only is this a fun and creative endeavor for our brands, but it’s an authentic way to strengthen our connection with customers,” said Stephen Budd, CEO of Sunwin Global Industry . “We look forward to sharing the insights gained through this pop-up with our broader customer base as we continue to evolve the products we develop for our partners—and as we expand the presence of our own brands across North America.” The shop is located on Hudson’s main drag at 316 Warren Street, two doors up from the lauded Maker Hotel and down the block from both Mel The Bakery and the Meat Hook along a corridor with ample foot traffic. Budd, who has had a weekend home in Hudson for many years, noted the town is filled with chic boutiques and hotels. “The stores in town are all so curated,” he added. “Hudson does not allow any retailer who has more than two locations.
