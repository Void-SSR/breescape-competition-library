"SHIP DATE" AND EXPRESS SHIPMENTS While we make every effort to ship your order as quickly as possible, often times an order will not ship on the same date as when the order is placed. Thus your "Order Date" and your "Ship Date" may not be the same. When you place an order, you will see an "Estimated Ship Date" on the order page. If you have selected an express shipping method, please be aware that the shipping speed will be in relation to the date when the order ships. For example, if you place an order on Friday evening and select 2 Day Shipping, your order may not ship until Monday and then will arrive on Wednesday. Although most orders are shipped the same or next day, please take weekends and holidays into account when anticipating shipping speed. SHIPPING TO P.O.
